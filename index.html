Research

I develop data-driven methods for understanding buildings at urban scale, from reconstructing accurate 3D geometry with UAV imagery to translating building information into energy models and decision-support tools.

Urban 3D Reconstruction UAV photogrammetry, computer vision, geometric modeling, and city-scale building reconstruction.
Urban Building Energy Modeling Scalable UBEM workflows, prototype buildings, calibration, and retrofit assessment.
Building Information Acquisition Remote sensing, multi-source urban data, machine learning, and automated model generation.

Skills: TypeScript, Cesium, Python, Ruby, AnyLogic, LaTeX, EnergyPlus, ArcGIS, and QGIS.

Publications

Selected Papers

Energy-saving potential of cool roofs at the urban scale: A case study of Xiamen city
PDF JCR Q1 · IF 8.0
Chengcheng Song, Yixing Chen*
Energy and Buildings, 2025.

This work develops a UBEM framework that integrates satellite-derived roof reflectance to evaluate the energy-saving potential and economic viability of cool-roof retrofits across Xiamen.

CityEL: A web-based platform to support city-scale building energy efficiency based on AutoBPS
PDF JCR Q1 · IF 13.3
Chengcheng Song, Jingjing Yang, Zhiyuan Wang, Ruoheng Li, Xiufeng Pang, Yixing Chen*
Sustainable Cities and Society, 2025.

CityEL (City Energy Lab) is a web-based platform for urban building energy efficiency. Unlike traditional UBEM tools, it focuses on rapid modeling and neighborhood-scale analysis, using GIS and AutoBPS to streamline energy benchmarking, retrofit evaluation, and cost analysis.

Developing urban building energy models for Shanghai city with multi-source open data
PDF JCR Q1 · IF 13.3
Chengcheng Song, Zhang Deng, Wenxian Zhao, Yue Yuan, Mengyue Liu, Shen Xu, Yixing Chen*
Sustainable Cities and Society, 2024.

This work integrates multi-source open data to model 609,000 building footprints in Shanghai. Machine learning classifies 63 building prototypes with 95% accuracy and supports city-scale energy simulation.

An emergency aircraft evacuation simulation considering passenger overtaking and luggage retrieval
PDF JCR Q1 · IF 13.7
Chengcheng Song, Quan Shao*, Pei Zhu, Min Dong, Wenfei Yu
Reliability Engineering & System Safety, 2023.

This work models cabin evacuation with cellular automata and quantifies the effects of luggage retrieval and overtaking. The results highlight the value of effective crew guidance for evacuation safety.
